# How to run: CC-Demo club system, all seven modules (full build v08, NEARCC look)

A working prototype of the whole CC-Demo club management system, version 8: the final scope of version 7 (every module live) in the look of the NEARCC Brand Guideline (violet and cool greys, Lato with a League Gothic wordmark, soft cards, both themes; v08 changes the look only): the Membership module (applications and every membership request, approval by a second person, fee payment confirmed in Finance, the member's own portal), Club Reporting (a report catalogue by role, report runs, Excel and PDF export, club-defined reports, a management dashboard and a finder for the old report names), Golf (tee sheets, portal and counter booking, an automated waitlist, check-in, course control, rain checks, handicaps, the shift close and the 12 golf reports), Events (setup and publication, online and counter registration, waitlist with offers, check-in and attendance, cancellations, rescheduling and authorised refunds, function-room synchronisation), Facilities (an availability grid with resource blocks, counter and online booking, amendment and cancellation with the 24-hour refund rule, exceptional refunds, no-shows, a waiting list, pool and Fitness Centre admission with day passes, the Bowling counter and its reconciliation, configuration as Draft until approved, Personal Training and 8 Facilities reports) and Resorts (reservations from the front desk and the Portal with groups and holds, amendment and cancellation under the 21-day rules, a waitlist by staff choice, no-shows, check-in and check-out with the folio, vehicles and door PINs, housekeeping and maintenance blocks, refunds and corrections approved by the Manager, Night Audit, the resort audit log and 5 Resorts reports; every resort rule is a draft for validation) and Finance (FIN-OPS, new in v07: one financial event for every money step of the other modules, validated and posted with Output GST; member accounts, receivables, payments with allocation, statements; standing orders and the GIRO run; spending credit; refunds by two people; deferred revenue and recognition; entrance-fee amortisation; reconciliation, clearing and the cashier close; adjustments, credit overrides and periods; Yonyou posting runs and responses; financial exceptions; dunning with escalation to Membership; General AR; Finance configuration under maker-checker; 10 Finance reports; rules marked *Interim design – pending Finance validation* where Finance has not confirmed them). Built against PRD v9 (the stories of PRD v8 unchanged). All data is invented.

## What you need
- A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Nothing to install, no server, no internet.

## Start it
A static site: it works offline, with the Lato font in `mockups/assets/fonts/` and Bootstrap 5.3.3 from a local copy in `mockups/vendor/`.
1. Open the `mockups/` folder. Keep it whole: `vendor/`, `assets/`, `css/`, `js/` and `data/` must stay next to `index.html`.
2. Double-click `index.html`. It opens in your browser on the **Launcher**.

## Use it
- **Switch persona / role**: there is no login. On the Launcher, open **Open a role** and click **Open tab** for a role; each role opens in its own browser tab and keeps it (the header shows the role). For a member or a non-member account, open the **Members** panel under *Open a role* and click **Open tab** next to it. Personas: Officer O. Lee (Membership Officer), Approver P. Lim (LB10002), Member A. Tan (TA10001), Member T. Chan (CT10020), non-member accounts H. Lim, P. Rao, S. Wong, J. Teo (NM-01 – NM-04).
- **Header**: the Manager and the Administrator find Tee Sheet, Course Control, Golfers and Golf Admin under the **Golf** menu, and Front desk, Rooms, Night Audit and Resort config under the **Resorts** menu, and Finance home, Financial events, Member accounts and Dunning (read only) under the **Finance** menu; Golf Operations, the Resort Front Desk and the two Finance roles keep their own links.
- **Reports**: in an Officer, Golf Operations, Facilities Staff, Manager or Administrator tab, click **Reports** in the header. In the Report Catalogue the column **Export as** has a **PDF** and an **XLS** button for each exportable report: one click runs it with its default parameters and exports it (XLS downloads; PDF opens the report page and the print dialog), recorded as a run. Or open a report, set its parameters and click **Run**, then **Export Excel** or **Export PDF**. Facilities Staff see the 8 Facilities reports (HMS-FAC-RR-001 – 008); the Resort Front Desk sees the 5 Resorts reports (HMS-RES-RR-001 – 005); the Finance roles, the Manager and the Administrator see the 10 Finance reports (FIN-OPS-RR-…).
- **Golf**: the **Golf Operations** tab for the tee sheet, bookings, waitlist, check-in, course control, golfers and the shift close; the **Manager** tab for golf approvals and course suspension; the **Administrator** tab for Golf Admin; a **Member** tab books tee times in the portal.
- **Events**: the **Events Team** tab for the schedule, event setup (the venue is chosen from the Facilities records; publishing blocks a bookable venue), each event, **Check-in** and **Function Rooms**; the **Golf Operations** tab registers walk-ins; the **Manager** tab authorises event refunds; a **Member** or non-member tab registers in the Portal.
- **Facilities**: the **Facilities Staff** tab for **Facilities** (availability grid by facility and date; **Book** in a free slot; blocks), **Bookings** (find a booking; the **Waiting list** tab with **Let offer expire**), **Admissions** (Swimming Pool and Fitness Centre; day passes; Personal Training) and **Bowling** (counter sales; **Import the sample export** to reconcile; exceptions). The **Manager** tab approves exceptional refunds and Draft configuration (Approvals → *Facility refunds*, *Facility configuration*; **Approve** is shown before **Reject**); the **Administrator** tab has **Facilities Admin** (facilities, resources, changes saved as Draft, Fitness Centre lists); a **Member** or non-member tab books, amends, cancels and joins the waiting list in the Portal (*Facilities*).
- **Resorts**: the **Resort Front Desk** tab for **Front desk** (arrivals, departures, in-house, reservations and the resort **Waitlist**; *New reservation*, *Group*, *Hold*; the rooms × nights grid), each reservation (amend, cancel, **Pay hold**, **Let hold expire**, **Process no-show**), **Open stay** (check in, folio, charges and discounts, credit notes and adjustments for the Manager, extend, move room, vehicles, door PIN, **Take payment**, **Check out**, reversal) and **Night Audit**; the **Housekeeping** tab for **Rooms** (Start cleaning, Mark ready, Faulty or Maintenance Blocked, Record completion); the **Manager** tab approves resort refunds, exceptions (credit notes, adjustments, late arrivals, reversals) and Draft configuration (Approvals → *Resort refunds*, *Resort exceptions*, *Resort configuration*) and closes the business day; the **Administrator** tab has **Resort config**; a **Member** or non-member tab reserves, pays the deposit, amends, cancels and joins the waitlist in the Portal (*Resorts*). Facilities Staff admit a resort guest to the Fitness Centre with the reservation number of an in-house stay. Every resort page shows *Draft – for validation*: the resort rules and values are demo values from a draft source pack.
- **Finance**: the **Finance Officer** tab lands on **Finance home** (KPI tiles, today's events by module, the posting runs, the *Finance available* switch) with a sub-navigation: **Financial events** (the register and the **Validate queue**; each event shows its posting set), **Member accounts** (receivables, **Record payment** with allocation, **Issue statement**, reprints), **Collections** (**Run October collection**, **Apply bank results**, **Apply returned cheques**), **Refunds and adjustments** (prepare refunds, deposit refunds and adjustments; credit-limit overrides), **Revenue recognition** (spending credit, **Recognise delivered events**, amortisation), **Reconciliation** (cashier sessions, reconciliations, the exception register), **Periods and posting** (the 13:00 and 21:00 runs, export and responses, periods), **Dunning**, **General AR** (debtors, invoices, credit notes, recurring billing) and **Finance configuration** (changes saved as Draft). The **Finance Approver** tab approves what the Finance Officer prepared in **Finance approvals** (refunds, adjustments, overrides, invoices and credit notes, configuration): the person who prepared an item can never approve it. The **Manager** and the **Administrator** read the Finance pages without actions. Pages that use a rule Finance has not confirmed show *Interim design – pending Finance validation*.
- **Demo clock**: Golf, Events and Facilities run on a fixed demo clock, **Wed 28 Oct 2026 11:55**; resort pages have a **Front desk time** switch on the same day, **09:30** (departures) or **15:30** (arrivals); the demo public holiday is Mon 2 Nov 2026 (weekend rates). Finance runs on the same day: the posting runs, the GIRO collection, recognition and amortisation start from their buttons. **Let offer expire** ends a waitlist or waiting-list offer, **Let hold expire** releases event places or resort rooms held for payment.
- **Module details**: on the Launcher, each live module card has **Module details**, which opens the module's workflow, roles, rules and scenarios and scrolls to them.
- **Follow a scenario**: on the Launcher, open **Module details** (after Modules), choose a module's tab and click **Start** next to a scenario; the launcher's own scenarios are on the *Launcher* tab. It opens in its first role's tab with the **Guide** panel beside the page listing every step; use the guide's **Open … tab** links when the role changes, and **End guide** to stop.
- **Start again with clean data**: on the Launcher, open **Demo data** and click **Reset demo data**.
- **Save or share the data**: **Export data** downloads `ccdemo-data-<date>.json`; **Import data** loads such a file back (any other file is refused and nothing changes).
- **Where the data lives**: your browser's local storage, key `nsrcc_prototype-full-v08`. The original seed data is in `mockups/data/seed.json` (and `seed.js`, which the pages load). The light or dark theme you choose is remembered under `ccdemo-theme`.
- **Payments**: every payment, charge and refund of every module is a financial event in Finance (FIN-OPS, references `FE-…`, refunds `RFD-…`), shown on the module's record and in the Integration feed. Membership fees are confirmed by an Approver in Approvals → *Fee payments* (**Confirm payment** or **Mark payment failed**); golf, event and resort charges to a member account go to the member's receivables; online and counter payments use **Pay (simulated): Success / Decline** in place of the card or PayNow provider; a refund approved in its module then needs the Finance Officer to prepare it and the Finance Approver to approve it. No money moves: the bank, Yonyou, the payment provider and the POS file are simulated.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The page is blank, or the data does not load | Make sure `data/seed.js` exists next to `index.html`, and open `index.html` itself, not a copy elsewhere |
| The page has no styling (plain text and blue links) | The `vendor/` folder is missing: copy the whole `mockups/` folder, not single files |
| A page shows only "This page is for …" | The tab has the wrong role, or was not opened from the Launcher: open the page from the Launcher in a tab for that role |
| A page shows "Unknown event …", "Unknown booking …", "Unknown facility …", "Unknown reservation …", "Unknown stay …", "Unknown financial event …" or "Unknown member account …" | The link names a record that does not exist: open it from the Events schedule, the tee sheet, the Facilities page, the Resort front desk, the Financial events register or Member accounts |
| My changes disappeared | Browser storage was cleared, or you used a private window. Use **Export data** to keep a copy |
| The data looks wrong after testing | Click **Reset demo data** |

## Limits
- **Screen sizes:** desktop-first; below 992px tables show as cards and nothing scrolls sideways.
This is a prototype: no real login (role tabs), invented data, no real e-mail or SMS (notices go to an outbox), no real money (Finance records every financial event, but the bank files, Yonyou, the card and PayNow provider and the POS file are simulated), other systems (the function-room booking system, the Bowling lane system, the door-access PINs and the carpark whitelist) simulated, SAFRA card numbers not verified, a fixed demo clock and the resort Front desk time switch, resort rules and values from a draft pack and Finance values marked *Interim design* or TBC (not NSRCC policy), late-payment interest off, one browser at a time. Excel export is an .xls file built in the browser (Excel shows a format notice) and PDF export is the browser's print. See [handover/handover.md](handover/handover.md) for details.
