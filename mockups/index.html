CC-Demo

A working demo of a club management system, all seven modules: the Membership module (applications and every membership request, approval by a second person, payment, the member's own portal), Club Reporting (a report catalogue by role, report runs, export, club-defined reports and a finder for the old report names), Golf (tee sheets, online and counter booking, an automated waitlist, check-in, rain checks and the shift close), Events (setup, registration for members, dependants and non-members, waitlist, check-in, cancellations and refunds), Facilities (bookings with blocks and a waiting list, pool and gym admission with day passes, the Bowling counter and reconciliation, Personal Training) and Resorts (reservations with groups and holds, the 21-day rules, a waitlist by staff choice, check-in and check-out with folio, vehicles and door PINs, housekeeping, Night Audit and 5 reports; every resort rule a draft for validation) and Finance (one intake of every module's financial events, postings with GST, receivables and payments, GIRO, refunds approved by a second person, revenue recognition, reconciliation, Yonyou posting runs, dunning and General AR; interim rules marked), for the existing members. The payment provider, the bank, Yonyou and the other systems are simulated in the integration feed. All data is invented.

Quick start: open Module details, choose a module and click Start on a scenario. It opens in its role's tab with the guide beside the page; use the guide's Open … tab links when the role changes. Reset the demo data before each scenario.

Resorts

Draft – for validationResort rules and values are demo values from a draft source pack (D-348).

Workflow

  1. 1 Reserve: single, group or hold, eligibility from Membership
  2. 2 Pay the deposit (simulated), amend or cancel under the 21-day rules
  3. 3 Check in on the arrival date, in a Ready room
  4. 4 In-house folio, vehicles and door PIN
  5. 5 Check out with a settled folio; housekeeping
  6. 6 Night Audit closes the day

The resort waitlist is chosen by staff, unlike the Golf, Events and Facilities waiting lists (RC-122).
